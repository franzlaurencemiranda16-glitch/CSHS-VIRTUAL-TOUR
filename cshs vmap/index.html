About the project

About the CSHS virtual tour

Cabiao Senior High School (CSHS) is located in Cabiao, Nueva Ecija, Philippines. This interactive virtual tour and campus map was built so students, parents, and visitors can explore the campus online before ever setting foot on it.

Use the campus map to search for any office or classroom, or step directly into the 360° virtual tour to walk the halls yourself. Everything here — from the map pins to the panoramic scenes — is kept up to date by the school's own content team through the admin dashboard.

Getting started

1. Delete the sample DEMO DATA locations in Locations and add CSHS's real classrooms and offices.
2. Upload the real campus map (a photo, satellite image, or site plan) in Map management.
3. Upload a 360° panorama for each location and connect them with hotspots in Virtual tour.
4. Update the homepage headline, description, and stats in Site content.
